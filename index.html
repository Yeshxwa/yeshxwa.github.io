Hi, I’m Joshua—I build the "feel" of games.

I’m an aspiring Gameplay Programmer with a passion for turning complex logic into smooth, interactive experiences. I spend my days bridging the gap between clean C# code and that 'perfect' gameplay feel—usually with a fresh cup of coffee in hand. I value stability and creative expression in my work, whether I'm architecting systems in Unity or deep-diving into new technologies. I’m a firm believer that the best games are built with a mix of technical precision and a curious mind.
